## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account
To access the full suite of features—such as saving cafes, logging visits, and splitting bills—you need to create an account.
1. Navigate to the homepage.
2. Click **Join Community** or **Sign Up** in the navigation bar.
3. Enter your Name, Email, and a secure password.
4. Once registered, you will be redirected to your authenticated dashboard.

### The Dashboard
Your dashboard is your personalized command center. From here, you can quickly jump into four main actions:
- **Log Your Visit**: Record a new cafe experience.
- **Browse Cafes**: Open the interactive map and directory.
- **Split the Bill**: Distribute expenses seamlessly.
- **Community**: See where your friends are exploring.

## 2. Discovering Cafes

The **Discover** page is the heart of CafeHop. It features a curated list of sanctuaries perfect for deep work, casual catch-ups, or reading.

### Using the Map and Directory
- **Interactive Map**: Pan and zoom around the map to see cafe markers. Clicking a marker will open a quick-action popup showing the cafe's photo, rating, and immediate actions (like getting directions or logging a visit).
- **Directory Sidebar**: Scroll through the list of cafes on the left. Hovering or clicking a card will highlight the corresponding pin on the map.
- **Get Directions**: Click the navigation icon on any cafe card to instantly open walking directions in Google Maps.

### Searching and Filtering
- **Search Bar**: Type a cafe name, area (e.g., "Thamel"), or a specific vibe.
- **Mood Chips**: Below the search bar, click on moods like **Work Focus**, **Chill**, **Social**, or **WiFi** to instantly filter the map. You can combine multiple tags.
- **Sorting**: Use the sort dropdown in the sidebar to order results by **Rating (Highest)**, **Name (A-Z)**, or **Nearest** (if location is enabled).

## 3. Logging Your Visits

Keep track of where you've been and help the community by leaving helpful logs. 

1. On any cafe card, click the **Log Visit** button.
2. Select the specific cafe from the dropdown (if not already pre-filled).
3. **Rate the Cafe**: Leave a star rating (1-5).
4. **Vibe & Details**: Write a short note about your experience, evaluate the WiFi speed (Fast, Average, Poor), and rate the plug availability.
5. **Photos**: Upload photos of the ambiance or the menu.
6. Click **Submit**. 

*Note: The overall rating of a cafe on the platform is automatically calculated based on community logs!*

## 4. Community & Social Features

CafeHop isn't just a directory; it's a social network for explorers.

### Activity Feed
Navigate to the **Community** tab to see a real-time feed of logs and reviews from users across the platform.
- **Like & Comment**: Engage with other users' logs. Ask them about the WiFi speed or compliment their photos!
- **Follow Users**: Find users with similar taste and click **Follow** on their profile to prioritize their activity in your feed.

### Notifications
The bell icon in the sidebar keeps track of your social interactions. You will receive a notification when:
- Someone follows you.
- Someone likes or comments on your log.
- A user you follow posts a new visit.
- **Payment Alerts**: You are added to a split bill or a debt is settled.

## 5. Managing Expenses (Split the Bill)

CafeHop includes a powerful, Splitwise-inspired bill management system to make group outings stress-free.

### Creating a Split Bill
1. Navigate to the **Split Bill** section.
2. **Details**: Enter the total amount and select the cafe visited.
3. **Participants**: Search and add other CafeHop users.
4. **Split Logic**: The system automatically calculates equal splits, but you can adjust individual amounts if needed.
5. **Payment Mode**: Note how the bill was originally paid (Cash, eSewa, etc.).

### Tracking Balances
The **Balances** tab provides a high-level overview of your financial relationships:
- **Net Debt**: See exactly how much you owe or are owed by each friend across all bills.
- **Total Balance**: A summary of your overall standing (e.g., "You are owed Rs. 500 total").

---

## 6. Settling Debts & Digital Payments

Once a bill is split, you can settle your debts directly through the platform.

### Settle Up
1. In the **Balances** tab, click **Settle Up** next to a friend's name.
2. Choose to record a cash payment or use **Digital Payment**.

### eSewa Integration
CafeHop is integrated with **eSewa** for seamless digital settlements.
1. When settling a bill, select **eSewa** as the payment method.
2. You will be redirected to the secure eSewa portal to complete the transaction.
3. **Automatic Verification**: Once payment is successful, the platform automatically verifies the transaction and updates the bill status.
4. **Notifications**: Both the sender and receiver will receive a notification confirming the settlement.

## 7. Collections and Saved Lists

Never forget a recommendation again. You can create curated lists of cafes (e.g., "Best Work Spots", "Date Night").

1. On any cafe card on the Discover page, click the **Save** (Bookmark) icon.
2. A modal will appear. You can either select an existing list to add the cafe to, or type a name to create a **New List** on the fly.
3. To view your lists, go to your **Profile** page and navigate to the "Saved Lists" section.

## 8. Account & Profile

### Your Public Profile
Your profile acts as your cafe passport. It displays:
- All the logs you've written.
- Your earned badges (awarded for frequent logging and community engagement).
- Your follower/following count.
- **Edit Identity**: Click "Edit Profile" to update your bio, location, and profile avatar.

### Settings
To log out, click the **Sign Out** button at the very bottom of the main navigation sidebar.

## 9. Administrator Controls

Users with **Admin** privileges have access to a specialized dashboard for platform maintenance.

### Managing Nodes (Cafes)
- **Add/Edit Cafes**: Keep the directory up to date by adding new cafes or updating details for existing ones.
- **Verification**: Admins can mark cafes as "Verified" once their information (website, contact) is confirmed.

### User & Content Moderation
- **User Management**: Monitor user accounts and handle administrative actions if necessary.
- **Community Moderation**: Purge logs that violate community standards or contain inappropriate content.
- **Activity Monitoring**: View real-time traffic and visit logs across the platform.

### Financial Oversight
- **Ledger Access**: View all split bills and transaction records to ensure system integrity.
- **Revenue Tracking**: Monitor the total value of transactions processed through the platform.
